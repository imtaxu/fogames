⚡ FoGames: Agent Micro-Breaks

FoGames is designed to keep your flow state intact during those gaps while your AI agent (Cursor, Windsurf, etc.) is thinking or generating code.

Built with strict rules: Zero idle RAM when hidden, 100% privacy, and fully state-preserved gameplay.

🎮 We Need Your Games!

Missing your favorite micro-game? Have an idea for a simple 15-second distraction? We welcome Pull Requests and feature requests!
