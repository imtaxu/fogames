# FoGames: Agent Micro-Breaks

**Keep your flow while waiting for your AI agent to write code.**

Cursor, Windsurf, Antigravity, Kiro or Copilot... We all love AI agents. But what happens during those times when the agent is "thinking" or generating a massive refactor?  

You pick up your phone. You open Twitter, TikTok, or Instagram. Your context is shattered, and your flow state is gone.

**FoGames** is a zero-friction, ultra-lightweight VS Code extension designed specifically for these micro-breaks. It lives in your Activity Bar and offers instant, state-preserved micro-games to keep your brain engaged without breaking your coding context.

## Features

* **Zero-Latency:** No loading screens, no heavy frameworks. Built with pure Vanilla JS/HTML.
* **State Preserved:** Close the panel mid-game to check your code. When you open it back up, your 2048 grid or Tetris block is exactly where you left it.
* **Zero Idle RAM:** The Webview is fully disposed of when hidden. It consumes **0% CPU and RAM** while you are actively coding.
* **100% Privacy:** No external tracking, no analytics.

## The Games (MVP)
1. **2048:** The classic puzzle. Perfect for state-preserved, asynchronous thinking.
2. **Dino Run:** The ultimate offline reflex test. 
3. **Minimal Tetris:** Drop a few blocks while your code compiles.
4. **Flappy Bug:** Clear your mind with a pure reflex micro-game.
